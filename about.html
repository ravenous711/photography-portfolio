Process & Gear

How I Work

Cameras

Sony A7R V for digital, Contax G2 for 35mm film. I believe the tool matters far less than the eye behind it.

Lenses

Primarily a 35mm and 85mm shooter. The 35 for street and travel, the 85 for portraits and compressed landscapes.

Post-Processing

Lightroom Classic for culling and global edits, Photoshop for the occasional detailed retouch. Film scanned on Plustek 8200i.
